# Payment plans

A payment plan lets a customer pay what they owe in monthly parts. The city decides every plan by hand.

## Asking

- **Online:** on their account page the resident picks how many monthly payments (2 to 12) and writes a sentence about why. The first payment is due a month later.
- **By phone or at the counter:** a billing clerk, customer service person or city administrator opens the account and presses **Set up a payment plan**. They can choose up to 24 payments and the first due date.

An account can have only one plan, or one request, open at a time. The plan covers the balance on the day it was asked for.

## Deciding

People with the **Approver** role see the requests under **Payment plans**. They can change the number of payments or the first date, then **Approve**, or **Decline** with a note the customer will see. The person who asked can never decide their own request. The customer gets an email either way.

## While a plan is running

- Every payment made after approval counts toward the plan, by card, bank or at the counter. The pay page offers "Pay my payment plan amount" first.
- **On track** (paid at least what was due so far): bills from before the plan get no late charge, and the amount still left on the plan is not counted as overdue on the shutoff list. New bills are still due as usual.
- **Behind**: late charges and the shutoff list treat the account normally again until the customer catches up.
- Staff can end a plan from the account page. A resident can take back a request that has not been decided yet.
